Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 19, 2026

Focus (also called Worky) is a personal productivity app: timers, daily to-dos, lists, a calendar, a budget, and an optional email digest. This page explains what data it touches and what happens to it. The short version: your data stays yours, it is used only to show you your own information, and nothing is sold or shared.

Data stored in the app

Your tasks, lists, timers, calendar entries, budget, theme, and settings are stored in your browser's local storage on each device you use. If you sign in with Google to enable sync, the same data is also stored in a Firebase Realtime Database (operated by Google) under your own account, so that your other devices can read it. Only you can read your data; the database rules allow access solely to the signed-in account that owns it.

Google Sign-In

Signing in uses Firebase Authentication with your Google account. Focus receives your Google account identifier and email address in order to associate your synced data with you. It does not receive your password.

Gmail access for the email digest

The email digest is optional and off by default. If you connect Gmail, Focus requests the gmail.readonly permission, which lets it read your mail but never send, modify, or delete anything. It is used only to:

  • read the emails from the last 24 hours (excluding spam and trash),
  • produce a short daily summary and a list of suggested tasks,
  • store that summary — not the emails themselves — with your synced data so you can read it on any device.

Summaries are generated by an open-source language model that we run ourselves, either on your own computer or on a temporary cloud worker that is discarded after each run. Your email content is not sent to any third-party AI service. Email bodies are processed in memory and are not retained after the summary is produced.

Focus's use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. In particular, Gmail data is used only to provide the digest feature you enabled; it is never used for advertising, never sold, and never read by a human except with your explicit permission for support or as required by law.

What is not collected

  • No analytics, tracking pixels, or advertising identifiers.
  • No data is sold, rented, or shared with third parties.
  • No data is used to train machine-learning models unless you explicitly opt in to a future feature that says so.

Third-party services

Focus runs on GitHub Pages and uses Google Firebase for authentication and sync. Those providers process data on our behalf under their own privacy policies: GitHub, Firebase.

Retention and deletion

Your data is kept for as long as you use the app. You can delete it at any time:

  • Local data: clear the site's storage in your browser, or use the reset option in Settings.
  • Synced data: sign out and remove the app from your account, or email the address below to have your database record deleted.
  • Gmail access: revoke it at myaccount.google.com/permissions. Focus loses access immediately and no further digests are generated.

Changes

If this policy changes, the date at the top will be updated. Material changes to how Gmail data is handled will be announced inside the app before they take effect.
